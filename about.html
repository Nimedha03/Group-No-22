ABOUT THE PROJECT

BUILT BY BICT STUDENTS,
FOR BICT STUDENTS

DOCSHARE is the BICT Student Resource Hub — a single, organized place for lecture notes, tutorials, and past papers, built so no one has to dig through five different group chats to find one file.

THE PROBLEM

Notes, past papers, and tutorials for the Department of ICT are scattered across WhatsApp groups, shared drives, and personal folders. Resources get lost between batches, duplicated effort is common, and there's no easy way to search for a specific subject or course code when you actually need it.

THE FIX

DOCSHARE centralizes everything into one digital repository. Students upload structured resources under clear categories, browse by semester or subject, and search by course code, keyword, or resource type — so academic material stays organized and accessible across every batch in the department.

What DOCSHARE does

Three core features, built around how students actually study.

01 — UPLOAD & BROWSE

Share and find resources fast

Upload PDF documents or text notes and browse files organized by semester and subject, with clean download access for everyone.

02 — SEARCH & FILTER

Find exactly what you need

A dynamic search bar lets you filter resources by course code, keyword, or type — notes, past papers, tutorials — in seconds.

03 — ACCOUNTS & DASHBOARD

Manage your contributions

Secure login and registration let students manage what they've uploaded and keep track of saved or bookmarked materials.

Student Contributors

Students and class monitors who upload and organize helpful academic resources for their batch — keeping the hub current and useful for everyone coming after them.

Lecturers & Instructors

Instructors who want a single, reliable place to make reference materials easily accessible to students, without extra admin overhead.
